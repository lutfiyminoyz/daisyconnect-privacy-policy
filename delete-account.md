# Account and Data Deletion Request – DaisyConnect

This page explains how users of the **DaisyConnect** mobile application can request the deletion of their account and all associated personal data.

---

## How to Request Account Deletion

To request the permanent deletion of your DaisyConnect account and associated data, please submit our official Account Deletion Request Form:

👉 **[Submit an Account Deletion Request (Google Form)](https://forms.gle/mgkTx3FyFuKoHfGAA)**

### Steps to Complete the Request:
1. Open the Google Form link above (no app login required).
2. Enter the **email address** and **full name** associated with your DaisyConnect account.
3. Check the confirmation box acknowledging that account deletion is permanent.
4. Click **Submit**.

We will process your request, permanently delete your account and associated data within **7 business days**, and send a confirmation email to your registered address once completed.

---

## What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained?

When your deletion request is processed, the following data is **permanently deleted** from our Supabase database and servers:
* **Account Information:** Your name, email address, user ID, and authentication credentials.
* **User-Generated Content:** All posts, comments, messages, uploaded photos, and profile details submitted within the residential community network.
* **Device Identifiers:** Your Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push notification token.

**Data Retention:** We do **not** retain any personal identifiable information after your account is deleted.
